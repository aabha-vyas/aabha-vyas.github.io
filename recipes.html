Recipe Recommender

CS 410content-based recommendation · Python, scikit-learn, sentence embeddings

Pick a recipe you like and it finds similar ones, then re-ranks them by what you care about: fewer ingredients, fewer steps or a healthier dish. It works from the recipes' own text across 25,000 recipes, with no user ratings needed.

It started as my CS 410 project, where I judged results by reading them. I rebuilt it as a machine learning project so every change is measured.

view the code

how it works

find similar recipes

Each recipe's title, ingredients and cooking steps become vectors. I compared three ways to build them (TF-IDF, LSA and pretrained sentence embeddings) and tuned how much each part counts on one set of recipes, then scored the result on a separate set.

re-rank and explain

The closest matches are re-ranked by your preference. Each result shows the ingredients it adds that your recipe doesn't have and a two-sentence summary of the steps.

The biggest gain came from tuning, not a fancier model: re-weighting the same TF-IDF features got most of the way there, and the tuned weights lean heavily on cooking steps. How a recipe is cooked separates a stew from a soup better than its ingredients do.

The health score is now a classifier that reads only the ingredients. It learned that butter, cheese and cream push a recipe away from health categories, and that vegetables, whole wheat and nonfat items push toward them.

limits

  • A match counts as relevant if it shares a subcategory with your recipe, which stands in for whether a person would like it.
  • The health score ranks recipes by how they're tagged. It isn't nutrition advice.
